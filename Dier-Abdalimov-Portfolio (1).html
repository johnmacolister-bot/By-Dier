About Me

Curious about how the world connects.

  • Based inKazakhstan
  • CurrentlyStudent, Grade 11

I am a 17-year-old student from Kazakhstan, currently in Grade 11. My interest in international affairs comes from a simple question: how do countries with different histories, interests and languages find ways to work together?

My academic interests include International Relations, diplomacy and foreign policy, international politics, global cooperation, European affairs, cross-cultural communication and international education. I enjoy following current events and trying to understand the institutions and decisions behind them.

Kazakhstan's position between regions makes questions of regional cooperation and foreign policy feel close to home. I see diplomacy as, at its core, patient communication between people who see the world differently, and that is the skill I most want to develop.

My long-term ambition is to pursue higher education abroad and eventually build a career connected to international affairs and diplomacy. This website is a place to document that journey as it develops: what I read, what I learn and what I create.

Areas of Interest

What I am studying and exploring.

Five themes guide my reading and independent learning.

01

International Relations

How states, organisations and people interact, and the theories that try to explain cooperation, competition and conflict.

02

Diplomacy

The craft of dialogue and negotiation: how disagreements are managed and agreements are built between nations.

03

Foreign Policy

How countries define their interests and choose their partners, with particular attention to Kazakhstan and its region.

04

European Affairs

European integration, institutions and partnerships, and what they reveal about long-term cooperation between neighbours.

05

Global Cooperation

International institutions and shared challenges, and how countries coordinate when no single country can solve them alone.
